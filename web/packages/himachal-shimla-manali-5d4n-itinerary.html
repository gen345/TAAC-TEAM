Your Journey Plan

Chandigarh → Shimla → Kufri → Manali → Rohtang → Solang → Chandigarh

Day 01

Chandigarh → Shimla | Arrival · Toy Train Option · Mall Road

  • Option A — Toy Train: Drive to Kalka → board Kalka–Shimla Heritage Railway (UNESCO) · 5–6 hrs scenic mountain ride
  • Option B — Direct Drive: Chandigarh to Shimla (~3.5–4.5 hrs)
  • Arrive Shimla — check-in & freshen up
  • The Ridge — iconic open promenade with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Christ Church — one of North India's oldest churches; neo-Gothic architecture
  • Mall Road — Shimla's famous colonial-era shopping & strolling avenue
  • Scandal Point — famous historic landmark at Mall Road & The Ridge junction
🏨 Stay — Shimla
💡 Tip: Book toy train tickets well in advance. Mall Road is pedestrian only. The Ridge at sunset is spectacular.
Day 02

Fagu – Kufri – Naldehra Circuit | Snow Hills · Golf Course · Green Valley

  • Fagu — peaceful hamlet with stunning Himalayan panoramas & apple orchards
  • Kufri — popular hill resort with pony rides, yak rides & snow activities (seasonal)
  • Naldehra — site of one of India's oldest golf courses; serene cedar forest setting
  • Green Valley — scenic stretch of dense deodar forest; great photography stop
🏨 Stay — Shimla
💡 Tip: Start by 8 AM to cover all stops. Snow at Kufri is seasonal — confirm availability. Naldehra golf playing charges are extra.
Day 03

Shimla → Manali | Scenic Highway · Beas River · Kullu Valley (~7–8 hrs)

  • Depart Shimla by 7 AM sharp — early start non-negotiable
  • Gobind Sagar Lake (Bilaspur) — quick stop at the scenic reservoir
  • Pandoh Dam — impressive dam on the Beas River; great photo stop
  • Beas River & Kullu Valley viewpoints — lush valley with apple orchards & peaks
  • Arrive Manali by 4–5 PM — check-in & rest
  • Manali Mall Road — evening stroll through lively market & cafes
🏨 Stay — Manali
💡 Tip: 7 AM start is mandatory — 7–8 hr drive. Carry snacks and water. Keep valley stops brief.
Day 04

Rohtang / Solang / Atal Tunnel Circuit | Snow Pass · Adventure · Hot Springs

  • Rohtang Pass (13,058 ft) — high altitude snow pass; views of Lahaul & Spiti (permit required, weather dependent)
  • OR Snow Point before Rohtang — snow activities available if Rohtang is closed
  • Solang Valley — adventure hub; snow sports, zip-lining & paragliding (charges extra)
  • Atal Tunnel — world's longest highway tunnel at 3,000 m; cross to Sissu for Lahaul views
  • Vashisht Temple & Hot Springs — natural hot spring baths; perfect post-adventure relaxation
🏨 Stay — Manali
💡 Tip: Book Rohtang permit online via HP Govt portal in advance. Start by 6–7 AM — Rohtang closes early for return vehicles.
Day 05

Manali → Chandigarh | Departure Day (~7–8 hrs)

  • Hadimba Devi Temple — early morning visit if not covered; serene & uncrowded at dawn (30 mins)
  • Depart Manali for Chandigarh — scenic descent through Beas valley & Kullu
  • Arrive Chandigarh — tour ends with beautiful Himachal memories
✈️ Departure — Chandigarh
💡 Tip: Early start essential — 7–8 hr drive. Factor in extra time during peak season traffic on the highway.

Before You Go

Travel Tips & Essentials

🌤️ Best Time
March–June (snow/spring) and September–November (clear skies). Rohtang usually closed Nov–May.
🏔️ Rohtang Pass
Permit required — book online via HP Govt portal in advance. Weather dependent. Available on certain days only.
🚂 Toy Train
Kalka–Shimla toy train is a UNESCO heritage experience. Book tickets well in advance in peak season.
🚇 Atal Tunnel
World's longest highway tunnel connects Manali to Lahaul. Cross it for a quick Sissu valley view if time permits.
📶 Connectivity
Good signal in Shimla and Manali. Weak on the Shimla–Manali highway. Download offline maps.
🎒 Gear
Carry warm layers, woollen cap and gloves. Snow gear rental available at Rohtang/Solang if needed.
