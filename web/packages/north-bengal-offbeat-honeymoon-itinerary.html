Your Journey Plan

NJP / IXB → Kurseong → Darjeeling → Chota Mangwa → Santook → NJP / IXB

Day 01

NJP / IXB → Kurseong | Arrival & Hill Town Welcome

  • Pickup from NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport
  • Scenic winding drive into the hills — transfer to Kurseong (~2 hrs)
  • Hotel check-in & freshen up
  • Peaceful hill town vibes — leisurely evening stroll
  • Explore Kurseong town market & local cafes
🏨 Stay — Kurseong
💡 Tip: Kurseong is quieter and less commercialised than Darjeeling — ideal for a relaxed first evening. Keep the night easy after the travel day.
Day 02

Kurseong → Darjeeling | Queen of the Hills (~2 hrs via sightseeing stops)

  • After breakfast, begin Kurseong sightseeing
  • Dowhill Forest — mystical dense forest walk; one of Kurseong's most iconic romantic spots
  • Eagle's Crag View Point — sweeping valley & Himalayan mountain views
  • Tea Gardens — drive through lush green tea estate landscape
  • Transfer to Darjeeling & hotel check-in
  • Lunch in Darjeeling on own (not included in package)
  • Evening leisure at Mall Road — local shopping, street food & city vibe
🏨 Stay — Darjeeling
💡 Tip: Dowhill Forest has a mysterious, romantic atmosphere — couples love it. Mall Road evenings are best enjoyed on foot.
Day 03

Darjeeling Sightseeing → Chota Mangwa | Early Start Recommended

  • Tiger Hill — early morning sunrise over Kanchenjunga (optional, extra cost — jeep must be booked evening prior)
  • Batasia Loop — scenic spiral railway loop with Gorkha War Memorial
  • Ghoom Monastery — one of Darjeeling's oldest and most revered Buddhist monasteries
  • Peace Pagoda — Japanese-built white stupa with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Japanese Temple — serene temple complex near Peace Pagoda
  • Transfer to Chota Mangwa — peaceful orange village retreat
  • Enjoy orange village views, fresh air & a quiet romantic evening
🏨 Stay — Chota Mangwa
💡 Tip: Tiger Hill jeep booking must be arranged the evening before. Chota Mangwa is a hidden gem — guests are always pleasantly surprised. Carry a light jacket.
Day 04

Chota Mangwa → Santook | Offbeat Valley Drive & Mountain Escape

  • After breakfast, check-out and transfer to Santook
  • En-route visit Tinchuley — charming eco-village with Himalayan views
  • Kalimpong town drive — briefly explore this vibrant hill town
  • Deolo View Point — panoramic views over the Teesta valley & surrounding peaks
  • Arrive Santook — enjoy offbeat nature, mountain views & peaceful surroundings
🏨 Stay — Santook
💡 Tip: Santook is one of the most offbeat and romantically serene stays in North Bengal. Day 4 is leisurely — let the couple enjoy the mountain stillness.
Day 05

Santook → NJP / IXB | Departure Day

  • Morning — optional river bath experience at nearby stream
  • After breakfast, check-out from Santook
  • Transfer to NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport (~3–4 hrs)
  • Drop & departure — end of an unforgettable journey
✈️ Departure — NJP / Bagdogra
💡 Tip: Depart by 8 AM for afternoon trains or flights. Santook to NJP is approx 3–4 hrs. All sightseeing subject to time, weather & road conditions.

Before You Go

Travel Tips & Essentials

🌤️ Best Time
October–May. June is early monsoon — carry a light rain jacket. Mornings are usually clear with mountain views.
🌄 Tiger Hill
Tiger Hill jeep must be booked the evening prior. Start by 3:30–4:00 AM. On clear days the Kanchenjunga view is breathtaking.
🍽️ Meals
All meals included except Lunch on Day 2 in Darjeeling — explore Mall Road or Chowrasta for local food options.
📶 Connectivity
Signal can be weak in Chota Mangwa and Santook. Download offline maps before travel. Carry a power bank.
🎒 Gear
Light woolens / jacket, rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes, power bank. Even in summer, evenings in Darjeeling can be cold.
⚠️ Note
All sightseeing is subject to time, weather & road conditions. Flexibility is key in the hills.
