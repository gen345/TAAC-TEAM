Your Journey Plan

NJP / Bagdogra → Sittong → Dawaipani → Darjeeling → NJP / Bagdogra

Day 01

NJP / Bagdogra → Sittong | Arrival & Orange Village

  • Pickup from NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport — meet our representative
  • Scenic drive through the North Bengal foothills towards Sittong
  • Ahaldara View Point — stunning panoramic viewpoint over the rolling North Bengal hills
  • Sittong Orange Village — explore the peaceful Himalayan village known for its orange cultivation
  • Seasonal Orange Orchards — walk through the fragrant orchards (season permitting)
  • Himalayan Sunset — golden hour views over the valleys and hills
🏨 Stay — Sittong · MAP Plan (Breakfast & Dinner)
💡 Tip: Orange season in Sittong peaks between November and January — plan accordingly for the best orchard experience. The drive from NJP is about 3–4 hours.
Day 02

Sittong → Dawaipani | Tagore Trail & Himalayan Villages

  • After breakfast, check out and drive towards Dawaipani
  • Jogighat — scenic riverside stop along the drive; peaceful and largely undiscovered
  • Mungpoo — visit the historic Tagore House (Rabindranath Tagore's retreat) and the Medicinal Plant Garden
  • Tinchuley View Point — sweeping views of the Kanchenjunga range and surrounding valleys
  • Lamahatta Eco Park — serene pine forest park with mountain views; ideal for a peaceful walk
  • Arrive Dawaipani — check-in and evening leisure in this peaceful Himalayan village
🏨 Stay — Dawaipani · MAP (Breakfast & Dinner)
💡 Tip: Tagore House in Mungpoo is a must — carry your camera. Lamahatta Eco Park is best in the late afternoon light. Dawaipani evenings are cool and quiet.
Day 03

Dawaipani → Darjeeling | Queen of Hills Sightseeing

  • After breakfast, check out and drive towards Darjeeling
  • Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park — home to Snow Leopard, Red Panda and Himalayan Wolf
  • Batasia Loop — iconic spiral railway loop of the Toy Train; stunning Kanchenjunga backdrop
  • Ghoom Monastery (Yiga Choeling) — one of the oldest Tibetan Buddhist monasteries in Darjeeling
  • Japanese Peace Pagoda — serene hilltop pagoda with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Chowrasta Mall Road — the heart of Darjeeling; evening stroll, cafes and colonial charm
  • Darjeeling Local Market — tea, handicrafts, local produce and Tibetan artefacts
🏨 Stay — Darjeeling · MAP (Breakfast & Dinner)
💡 Tip: Book Tiger Hill for the next morning if you want the sunrise (extra charges apply — decide tonight). Mall Road is best explored in the evening with a warm cup of Darjeeling tea.
Day 04

Darjeeling → NJP / Bagdogra | Departure Day

  • Tiger Hill (Optional, extra charges) — early morning golden sunrise over Kanchenjunga and Everest; one of India's most iconic dawn views
  • After breakfast, check out and begin return journey
  • Lepchajagat — serene pine forest village on the outskirts of Darjeeling; peaceful and offbeat
  • Simana View Point — panoramic viewpoint on the India-Nepal border; sweeping valley views
  • Pashupati Market (Optional) — Indo-Nepal border market; local goods and cross-border shopping
  • Mirik Lake — scenic boating lake surrounded by orange orchards and tea gardens
  • Drop at NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport
✈️ Departure — NJP / Bagdogra · Breakfast Included
💡 Tip: Tiger Hill requires a very early start (4–4:30 AM) — confirm the previous evening. Factor in Mirik stop time and reach NJP/Bagdogra well before your departure.

Before You Go

Travel Tips & Essentials

🌤️ Best Time
October–January for clear skies and Kanchenjunga views. November–January for peak orange season in Sittong. Avoid June–August for heavy monsoon rainfall.
🍊 Sittong
Orange orchards peak between November and January. October offers clear skies and pleasant weather even if oranges are early-stage. Carry warm layers — evenings get cold.
🏔️ Tiger Hill
Optional — extra charges apply. Requires a very early start (4–4:30 AM). Best views Oct–Dec before the winter haze sets in. Book tickets the previous evening.
🚂 Toy Train
Darjeeling Toy Train joyrides are available from Darjeeling station (own cost, not included). Book in advance during peak season — seats fill fast.
🧥 Clothing
Carry warm woolens for Darjeeling and Dawaipani evenings. October nights can be cold at altitude. A light windcheater is useful for all days.
💵 Cash
Carry sufficient cash from NJP/Bagdogra. ATMs are available in Darjeeling but can run out during peak season. Sittong and Dawaipani have very limited or no ATMs.
